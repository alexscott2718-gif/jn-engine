The Fix

src/game/behaviors/behavior_resolver_inert.c + src/game/entities.c

Added vt_resolver_inert, a deliberately hidden, non-solid, side-effect-free native behavior for the three final rows. The important part is that this is not a guess at their eventual visuals or controllers. It preserves the existing resolver output while making the coverage decision visible to the engine and the generated catalog.
