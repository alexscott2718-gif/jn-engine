How to test (about a minute)

  1. Open the Boy Genius browser demo (desktop browser is best).
  2. Pick a level from the level dropdown, then a scene from the Cutscene dropdown, and press Play Cutscene.
  3. Watch the camera framing. Press Stop to end early, or pick another scene.
  4. See something wrong? Press B, click the object, describe it (below).

What "correct" looks like

Every scene uses one of three camera modes. None of them should snap, jitter, clip through the ground, or point away from the actor who is talking:

ModeWhat you should see
StaticCamera holds a fixed spot and looks at the framed actor — like a mounted security cam.
OrbitCamera sits at a fixed offset around the actor and frames them head-on; smooth, no spin.
DollyCamera eases in or out along a straight line during the shot (the most common mode). The push/pull should be slow and steady, never a jump.

Audio should match the shot: the right voice line plays, and the shot ends roughly when the line finishes. Multi-shot scenes should cut cleanly from one framing to the next.

Worth flagging

  • Camera ends up underground, inside a wall, or facing empty space.
  • A visible jump/snap in the camera mid-shot (not a deliberate cut between shots).
  • The wrong actor is framed, or the talking actor is off-screen.
  • Audio plays the wrong line, cuts off early, or the shot hangs long after the line ends.
  • An actor in the scene is in a frozen / wrong pose while talking.

Already known — please don't refile

  • Non-player actors don't animate during cutscenes yet (Goddard, Cindy, Friends stand still). Camera + audio are the focus right now; actor animation is the next slice.
  • Goddard's texture / mapping is a known visual issue tracked separately.
  • Cindy's location / path is incomplete and intentionally on hold.
  • No audio? Some browsers need a click first, or block autoplay — that's the browser, not the scene.

How to report

Reports go straight from the demo — no account, no install:

  1. Press B (or the QA button) to turn report mode on.
  2. Click the object that's wrong. A tag pins to the top-right.
  3. Click the tag, pick a category (PLC placement, ORI orientation, SCL scale, ANI animation, TEX texture, MIS missing, GFX glitch, OTH other), and type what's wrong.
  4. When done, export the session and send it in. Full walkthrough: 2-minute reporting guide.
Cutscene-camera reports: use PLC (camera in the wrong spot) or GFX (snap / clipping / wrong framing), and name the level + scene you picked from the dropdowns so it can be reproduced.
